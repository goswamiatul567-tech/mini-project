About This Project

This project was built to solve critical issues in the Mid-Day Meal scheme, including fake attendance, food wastage, and a lack of accountability. By providing a transparent, data-driven dashboard, we empower school administrators and government officials to verify exactly what is happening on the ground. Privacy is paramount; we detect people and plates, but not identities.
