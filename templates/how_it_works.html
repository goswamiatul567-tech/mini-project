The Problem: Corruption & Wastage

In the traditional Mid-Day Meal system, monitoring relies on manual paper records. This leads to critical issues:

  • Ghost Beneficiaries: Fake attendance records lead to funds being stolen.
  • Food Wastage: Inaccurate headcounts result in food shortages or excess waste.
  • No Verification: Authorities have no way to prove if a meal was actually served.
Slide 02 / 06

Our Solution: AI Vision

We replace manual counting with real-time Artificial Intelligence.

🎯 We use YOLOv8 Computer Vision to detect two things simultaneously: Students entering the line and the Meal Plates being served.

Slide 03 / 06

No Double Counting (Tracking)

A student might stand in the camera frame for 10 seconds. If we count every frame, one student becomes ten.

To solve this, we integrated a ByteTrack-style tracking algorithm. Every student is assigned a unique ID (e.g., Student #1, #2, #3). Even as they move, the AI remembers them and only counts them once.

Slide 04 / 06

Human-in-the-Loop Verification

We believe AI should assist humans, not replace them blindly.

When the AI detects the students and plates, it does not save automatically. The operator (teacher) must review the visual count on the dashboard and click "Confirm & Save". This prevents false detections from ruining the database.

Slide 05 / 06

The Integrity Check

Once saved, the system acts as an auditor. It compares three numbers:

1. Official Attendance
2. AI Student Count
3. AI Plate Count

If the AI sees 45 students but only 40 plates, the dashboard instantly flags an ERROR: 5 students missing meals, alerting the principal immediately.
