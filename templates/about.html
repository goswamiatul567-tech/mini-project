About The Project

An AI-powered solution for transparent and accountable mid-day meal distribution.

The Problem We Are Solving

The Mid-Day Meal Scheme is the world's largest school feeding program, serving over 118 million children in India. However, the system faces severe challenges: fake attendance records, food wastage, and a critical lack of real-time monitoring.

Without a transparent verification system, it is impossible to guarantee that every allocated meal actually reaches a student. Our project aims to bridge this gap using AI.

Our Solution

The Meal Monitoring System uses smart cameras and Computer Vision (YOLOv8) to detect students and food plates. It features a human-in-the-loop verification dashboard, ensures students aren't double-counted using tracking algorithms, and operates entirely offline, syncing data when the internet returns.
