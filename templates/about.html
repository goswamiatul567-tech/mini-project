About the Project

The Meal Monitoring System is an AI-powered solution designed to make school mid-day meal distribution more transparent, reliable and efficient. It uses smart cameras and artificial intelligence to monitor students receiving meals, count meal plates, and verify whether the food served matches the officially prescribed daily menu.

The system works completely offline, stores records locally, and automatically synchronizes data with the government dashboard whenever an internet connection becomes available.

Our goal is to ensure that every student receives their rightful meal and every meal is properly accounted for.
